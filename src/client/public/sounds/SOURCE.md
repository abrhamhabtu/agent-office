# NYSE opening bell recording

`nyse-opening-bell.mp3` is a 4.5-second excerpt from [NYSEBell.wav by tahur1976](https://freesound.org/people/tahur1976/sounds/380490/), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The excerpt was taken from the end of the recording so the final ring decays naturally, then encoded as MP3 for the office preview.
